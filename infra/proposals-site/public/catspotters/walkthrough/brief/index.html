The dashboard in a minute

The dashboard is Syros Cats' side of the app, at team.catspotters.com. It works on a phone or a laptop. You invite each person with a role, and cat guardians see only their own colonies.

  • QueueReports from the public. The person who sent one hears back.
  • Vet visitsRecord a whole clinic day at once; each cat's record and cost update themselves.
  • ReportsSterilisation progress, colony health, coverage and vet costs, as spreadsheets for funders.
  • SettingsInvite people, set the €60 vet price, and switch the giving button and cat facts on or off.

What we need from you

  1. Naming patchy cats

    We plan to merge black and dark grey into one colour, and ginger and cream into another, because they're easily confused. That changes which cats get called tortoiseshell or calico.

    The three options and three questions
    • A. Name tortoiseshells and calicos only; describe a brown tabby with ginger patches in plain words.
    • B. Name torbies too.
    • C. No names, only plain colours.
    1. Do your volunteers and vets say tortoiseshell, calico or torbie? Which?
    2. Would you rather see torbies named, even if a few sandy tabbies get it wrong?
    3. "Calico" or "Tortoiseshell and white"?
  2. Confirm reports go to admin@syroscats.com

    We'll tell you before one test report, as it emails real people.

  3. The rest of your team

    Names and emails, so we can invite them.

  4. Ask your testers to start testing again

    A lot has changed since 29 September. A fresh round on real phones will catch anything we've missed.

  5. Try "Check for matches" on an Android phone

    One test confirms the Android fix.
