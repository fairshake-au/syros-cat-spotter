Fact 8 · Leftovers aren't cat food

Reword
NowOnions, garlic and chocolate are poisonous to cats. Cooked bones, cured meat and salty leftovers can hurt them too. Leftovers aren't cat food.
ProposedOnions, garlic and chocolate are poisonous to cats. Cooked bones can splinter, and salty scraps can make them ill. Leftovers aren't cat food.

Why: the vet sources back onion, garlic, chocolate, sharp cooked bones and too much salt. None of them says cured meat is a problem for cats, so it comes out.

FrenchL’oignon, l’ail et le chocolat sont toxiques pour les chats. Les os cuits peuvent se briser en éclats, et les restes salés peuvent les rendre malades. Les restes ne sont pas de la nourriture pour chats.

Recommend: approve. Needs Syros Cats: yes. This fact is already on their sign-off list, so they should see the new words.

Sources: Merck Vet Manual (onion, garlic) · Cats Protection · International Cat Care (bones) · ASPCA (chocolate, salt). The Merck chocolate page doesn't mention cats, so I didn't use it for chocolate.

Fact 15 · The slow blink

Reword
NowTry a slow blink, from a distance. A 2020 study found cats often slow-blink back, and seem more at ease with people who do it.
ProposedTry a slow blink, from a distance. In a 2020 study, cats were more likely to slow-blink back at someone who slow-blinked first, and to come up to them.

Why: the paper found more slow-blinking back and more approaching. It never measured whether cats were "at ease". It was also small (18 cats in each test), so the new wording sticks to what happened.

Greek and French drafts

GreekΔοκίμασε ένα αργό ανοιγοκλείσιμο των ματιών, από απόσταση. Σε μια μελέτη του 2020, οι γάτες ανταπέδιδαν πιο συχνά το αργό ανοιγοκλείσιμο και πλησίαζαν πιο εύκολα όποιον το είχε κάνει πρώτος.

FrenchEssaie un clignement lent des yeux, de loin. Dans une étude de 2020, les chats répondaient plus souvent par un clignement lent, et s’approchaient plus volontiers de celui qui l’avait fait en premier.

Recommend: approve. Needs Syros Cats: no. Johnny gets the new Greek and French.

Source: Humphrey et al. 2020, Scientific Reports (read through the free PubMed Central copy).

Fact 17 · Not every purr is happy

Reword
NowCats purr when they're content, but also when they're stressed, hurt or giving birth. A purr can be a cat calming itself down.
ProposedCats purr when they're content, but also when they're stressed or hurt. A purr can be a cat calming itself down.

Why: three sources back stress, pain and self-soothing. None of them mentions giving birth, so that part goes.

Fact 18 · Kittens' eyes open blue

Reword
NowKittens' eyes open blue. Their real colour comes through over the first few months, so a blue-eyed kitten may grow up green or gold.
ProposedKittens' eyes open blue. Their real colour comes through in the weeks that follow, so a blue-eyed kitten may grow up green or gold.

Why: Cats Protection says the colour starts to change at about five weeks and is the adult colour by seven. "A few months" runs later than that. The new wording gives no exact age, so it can't be wrong.

Greek and French drafts

GreekΤα μάτια των γατακιών ανοίγουν γαλάζια. Το πραγματικό τους χρώμα φαίνεται μέσα στις επόμενες εβδομάδες, οπότε ένα γατάκι με γαλάζια μάτια μπορεί να μεγαλώσει με πράσινα ή χρυσά.

FrenchLes yeux des chatons s’ouvrent bleus. Leur vraie couleur apparaît dans les semaines qui suivent : un chaton aux yeux bleus peut grandir avec des yeux verts ou dorés.

Recommend: approve. Needs Syros Cats: no.

Sources: Cats Protection ageing chart · Cats Protection on eye colour.

Fact 2 · How fast it adds up

Small tweak
NowOne unsterilised female can have two or three litters a year, and her daughters can be pregnant themselves from as young as four months.
ProposedOne unsterilised female can have up to three litters a year, and her daughters can be pregnant themselves from as young as four months.

Why: both sources say "up to three litters". Neither says two is normal. The four months is right.

Greek and French drafts

GreekΜία μη στειρωμένη θηλυκή γάτα μπορεί να κάνει έως και τρεις γέννες τον χρόνο, και οι κόρες της μπορούν να μείνουν έγκυες ήδη από τεσσάρων μηνών.

FrenchUne femelle non stérilisée peut avoir jusqu’à trois portées par an, et ses filles peuvent elles-mêmes attendre des petits dès l’âge de quatre mois.

Recommend: approve. It's a small change and the only one I'd call optional. Needs Syros Cats: no.

Sources: International Cat Care · Cats Protection. Agriculture Victoria's fact sheet wouldn't open (it returned an access-denied error), so I didn't use it.

Things to ask Syros Cats

Fact 6 · What it costs

Flag, not changed

The fact says €50–60, which is the figure from the 1 September meeting. Syros Cats' own public vet-care page says neutering costs €35 to 50 per cat. I haven't touched the fact. When you ask them about SYC-592, ask which figure is right now, and whether the web page is out of date.

Source: Syros Cats, veterinary care, read 24 October.

Fact 4 · Back home afterwards

Ask about "exactly"

The fact says cats go back to exactly where they were found. Syros Cats' TNR diary says cats are kept overnight and released the next day. It doesn't say each one goes back to the exact spot. If they hesitate, "back to where they were found" is safer. The fact is already on their sign-off list.

Source: Syros Cats TNR diary.

Ask

"On Syros it's usually the left ear" rests only on the 1 September meeting. Alley Cat Allies uses the left ear but doesn't call it universal, and no Syros Cats page names an ear. It's already on their sign-off list. Keep it there.
