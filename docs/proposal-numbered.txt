   1  Syros has a large population of street cats and a small number of people caring for them. The gap between those two facts is information. Nobody knows how many cats there are, which have been sterilised, which are in trouble, or where the next effort would do the most good.

   2  Every summer, thousands of people walk the island with cameras in their pockets, photographing cats for the joy of it. This app turns that scattered affection into the record you are missing, and then turns the affection itself into support for your work.

   3  Here is the whole idea in one story. A visitor photographs a tabby in Ano Syros, learns the island has never met her, and names her Agatha. The sighting is filed with a photograph, a location and a date. Over the summer other people meet Agatha and confirm her, and one of them notices her ear is not clipped. In September your team, planning a trapping round, sees an unsterilised female with a reliable feeding spot. I

   4  The people it serves

   5  Visitors experience it as a game. They photograph cats, collect the ones they meet, name the new ones and see how they rank.

   6  Residents experience it as recognition. The cat somebody has fed for six years gets a name, a page and a history, and the person who feeds it is named on that page.

   7  Families staying for a season , such as those who come with Boundless Life, are a distinct group. They stay one to three months rather than four days, they range far beyond the harbour, and their children have structured time. They matter more than their numbers suggest, for reasons section 12 explains.

   8  Syros Cats and its volunteers experience none of the above. For your team this is a working tool: which cats are sterilised, which colonies need attention, which animal was just reported injured, and what you can put in front of a funder at the end of the year. Nobody on your team uses this for entertainment, and it is not designed as though they did.

   9  These needs fit together rather than competing. The public side has to stay effortless, because every record your team relies on comes from somebody enjoying themselves. The more enjoyable the app is, the more data you get, the better your reporting becomes, and the stronger the case for donating. Each part feeds the next.

  10  Two ideas that run through everything

  11  The first is citizen science , which means ordinary people, with no training, collecting real observations that an organisation then uses. Birdwatchers counting garden birds on one weekend a year is the best-known example, and their counts feed genuine population studies. A visitor photographing a cat in an alley is doing the same thing. That is what makes this app worth a school's time, not just a tourist's.

  12  The second is that raising engagement and donation income for Syros Cats is an explicit objective, not a side effect . A tourist who has met and named a cat gives more readily than one who has read a poster, and the app is built to convert affection for a specific animal into support for the organisation caring for all of them.

 116  Real science, done by citizen scientists, including children

 151  Who is responsible under GDPR

 152  Because Fair Shake would provide and operate the app for you as a managed service, responsibility divides. Data protection law distinguishes the organisation that decides why data is collected, called the controller, from the organisation that handles it on the first one's instructions, called the processor. The controller carries most of the obligations, and the processor carries a narrower set of its own.

 153  Most likely position Syros Cats Controller for the data collected for your work: guardians, feeders and volunteers. You decide why those records exist. Fair Shake Processor, operating the app on your instructions, and controller only for the little data that exists to run the service itself.

 154  In practice that means a short written agreement between us covering security, help with deletion requests, breach notification and the suppliers behind the service, with the data stored in the European Union, which the suppliers we propose all support.

 155  We are not lawyers, and this is our understanding rather than legal advice. The division above is the usual one, but Fair Shake makes many of the design decisions about what gets collected, which can affect the analysis, so it is worth confirming. The agreement is far easier to put in place before launch than after.
