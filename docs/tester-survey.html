Five questions for the Syros testers

The survey to send to the people who have been testing the app: what they managed to do, how hard it was, what broke, and the one thing to fix before October. Two minutes to answer, anonymous by default.

  • 5 questions
  • ~2 minutes
  • English + Greek
  • Anonymous
  • Sent via WhatsApp

The survey

Questions 1–3 measure what actually happened; 4 and 5 ask them to prioritise. The orange notes are for you, not for them.

1 Multi-selectRequired

Which of these did you manage to do?

Tick everything you got as far as. It is fine if that is only one thing.

  • Took a photo of a cat and recorded the sighting
  • Confirmed a cat the app already knew
  • Registered a brand new cat and named it
  • Looked through the cat catalogue
  • Looked at the map
  • Checked my points, badges or the leaderboard
  • Used it somewhere with no signal or bad signal
  • Used it in Greek
  • I opened it but never recorded a cat
  • I could not get it to open or install at all

Τι καταφέρατε να κάνετε;

Σημειώστε όλα όσα προλάβατε. Δεν πειράζει αν είναι μόνο ένα.

  • Φωτογράφισα μια γάτα και κατέγραψα την παρατήρηση
  • Επιβεβαίωσα μια γάτα που η εφαρμογή γνώριζε ήδη
  • Κατέγραψα μια καινούργια γάτα και της έδωσα όνομα
  • Περιηγήθηκα στον κατάλογο με τις γάτες
  • Είδα τον χάρτη
  • Είδα τους πόντους, τα βραβεία ή την κατάταξη
  • Τη χρησιμοποίησα σε σημείο χωρίς σήμα ή με κακό σήμα
  • Τη χρησιμοποίησα στα ελληνικά
  • Την άνοιξα αλλά δεν κατέγραψα ποτέ γάτα
  • Δεν κατάφερα καθόλου να την ανοίξω ή να την εγκαταστήσω

Why this oneHow far down the loop each tester actually got. Someone who never recorded a cat is telling you something completely different from someone who registered five — and their answers to 2–5 have to be read differently. The last two options are the ones you most need to count.

2 Single choice, 1–5Required

How easy was it to record one cat, from opening the app to finishing?

1Very hard 2Hard 3All right 4Easy 5Very easy

Πόσο εύκολο ήταν να καταγράψετε μια γάτα, από το άνοιγμα της εφαρμογής μέχρι το τέλος;

1Πολύ δύσκολο 2Δύσκολο 3Εντάξει 4Εύκολο 5Πολύ εύκολο

Why this oneThe single number you can track. Ask it again a month after launch and the difference tells you whether the October fixes worked. Words on every point, not just the ends, so a 3 means the same thing to every tester.

3 Multi-select+ long text, optional

Did any of these happen to you?

Tick anything you hit, even once.

  • It said there was no cat in the photo when there clearly was
  • It treated a cat I know is already in the app as a new cat
  • It suggested a cat that was obviously a different cat
  • The location was wrong, or it would not pick up my location
  • The camera or the photo would not work
  • It was slow, froze, or closed itself
  • I lost a sighting I thought I had saved
  • I was not sure what to do next
  • The wording was confusing, or the Greek was wrong
  • Nothing went wrong

Tell us what happened, in as much or as little detail as you like. Long text, optional.

Σας συνέβη κάποιο από τα παρακάτω;

Σημειώστε ό,τι συνέβη, ακόμα και μία φορά.

  • Είπε ότι δεν υπάρχει γάτα στη φωτογραφία, ενώ υπήρχε
  • Είπε «καινούργια γάτα» για γάτα που ξέρω ότι είναι ήδη καταγεγραμμένη
  • Μου πρότεινε μια γάτα που ήταν προφανώς άλλη γάτα
  • Η τοποθεσία ήταν λάθος, ή δεν έβρισκε πού ήμουν
  • Η κάμερα ή η φωτογραφία δεν δούλεψε
  • Ήταν αργή, κόλλησε, ή έκλεισε μόνη της
  • Έχασα μια καταγραφή που νόμιζα ότι είχε αποθηκευτεί
  • Δεν ήμουν σίγουρος/η τι πρέπει να κάνω μετά
  • Τα κείμενα ήταν μπερδεμένα, ή τα ελληνικά λάθος
  • Δεν πήγε κάτι στραβά

Πείτε μας τι έγινε, με όσες λεπτομέρειες θέλετε. Ελεύθερο κείμενο, προαιρετικό.

Why this onePain points, made countable. The list prompts memory — people forget failures they worked around — and the first three options separate the two matching errors that need different fixes: missing a known cat, and offering a wrong one. The free text is where the reproducible bug reports come from.

4 Short textRequired

If we could only fix or add one thing before the app goes live in October, what should it be?

One thing. The one that would make the biggest difference to you.

Short text, required.

Αν μπορούσαμε να διορθώσουμε ή να προσθέσουμε μόνο ένα πράγμα πριν βγει η εφαρμογή τον Οκτώβριο, τι θα ήταν;

Ένα πράγμα. Αυτό που θα έκανε τη μεγαλύτερη διαφορά για εσάς.

Σύντομο κείμενο, υποχρεωτικό.

Why this oneDesired improvements, already prioritised. A "what would you like to see?" question returns a wishlist you cannot act on; forcing one answer makes the testers do the ranking, and whatever three or four people name independently is your October list.

5 Single choice+ short text, optional

Would you recommend it to someone else on Syros who cares about the cats?

  • Yes — I already have
  • Yes
  • Not yet, but I would once it is finished
  • No

What would have to change for that to be a clear yes? Short text, optional.

Θα την προτείνατε σε κάποιον άλλον στη Σύρο που νοιάζεται για τις γάτες;

  • Ναι — το έχω κάνει ήδη
  • Ναι
  • Όχι ακόμα, αλλά θα το έκανα όταν ολοκληρωθεί
  • Όχι

Τι θα έπρεπε να αλλάξει για να γίνει ένα καθαρό «ναι»; Σύντομο κείμενο, προαιρετικό.

Why this oneThe only question that asks whether the app is worth anything to them. "Already have" is the strongest signal in the survey — it is a thing they did, not a thing they predict. And the follow-up text catches the blocker people will not volunteer anywhere else.

The message to send

For the Syros Cats WhatsApp group. Short, because it is being read on a phone while doing something else.

Hello everyone — thank you for testing the cat app these past weeks.

Five quick questions about how it went, around two minutes, and it is anonymous: [SURVEY LINK]

If something did not work, that is the most useful thing you can tell us — we are fixing things before it goes live in October. Please answer even if you only opened it once.

Thank you, Samuel

Γεια σας — ευχαριστούμε πολύ που δοκιμάσατε την εφαρμογή για τις γάτες αυτές τις εβδομάδες.

Πέντε γρήγορες ερωτήσεις για το πώς σας φάνηκε, περίπου δύο λεπτά, και είναι ανώνυμες: [ΣΥΝΔΕΣΜΟΣ]

Αν κάτι δεν δούλεψε, αυτό είναι το πιο χρήσιμο που μπορείτε να μας πείτε — διορθώνουμε πράγματα πριν βγει επίσημα τον Οκτώβριο. Απαντήστε ακόμα κι αν την ανοίξατε μόνο μία φορά.

Ευχαριστούμε, Σαμουήλ

Build sheet

Field types, in order, for whichever form tool you use.

QField typeRules
1Checkboxes, 10 optionsRequired · do not shuffle · no "other"
2Linear scale 1–5, labelled endsRequired · label every point in the question text
3Checkboxes, 10 optionsRequired · "Nothing went wrong" clears the rest
3bParagraph textOptional
4Short answerRequired
5Multiple choice, 4 optionsRequired
5bShort answerOptional
  1. Two forms, not one bilingual form. A language question at the top costs a question you do not have spare, and mixed-language option lists get mis-ticked. Send both links in the same WhatsApp message and merge the responses afterwards.
  2. No email field, no name field. Email capture is a designed, purposeful moment inside the app — bolting it onto a feedback form is how you teach people the app collects things quietly. If you want to follow up, add one optional line at the end saying so, and say what for.
  3. The Greek needs a native read before you send it. It is faithful, but a tester survey is the wrong place to discover a clumsy phrase, and the Greek and French review is already an open item.
  4. Leave it open a week, then chase once. A group like this answers in the first day or not at all; one reminder roughly doubles the count, a second one annoys people you need.

Reading the answers

  1. Count question 1 first. Everyone who ticked only "opened it but never recorded a cat" or "could not get it to open" is the number that matters most — that is the share of willing, motivated people the app lost at the door.
  2. Question 3's first three options are a matching score. Read them against the number of people who actually recorded cats, not against the total, or you will flatter the matching.
  3. Question 4 answers cluster or they do not. Three or more testers naming the same thing is a launch blocker; a scatter of singletons means the basics are sound and those go to the backlog.
  4. A low score in question 2 from someone who ticked plenty in question 1 is the most valuable respondent you have. They pushed through difficulty rather than giving up — go and talk to them directly.
