# SYC-443 implementation plan: team screens say a coat in the old words

**Spec:** `docs/specs/coat-colours-and-pattern.md` §15, approved by Samuel on 18 October 2026 ("approved"), with T1A–T6A.
**Ticket:** SYC-443 (child of SYC-398). Folds in **SYC-436** (guardian pages), because T1A gives guardians the team words.
**Status:** for Samuel's approval. Nothing is built until he approves.

## In plain English (for Samuel)

Two pieces of work, one after the other:

1. **Team pages (PR 1).** Every place a team page says a coat switches to the old words, from one shared set of rules:
   - the cat page's summary line and photo description;
   - the Appearance section;
   - the history;
   - the Colour check list;
   - the Cats grid cards;
   - the drawn cat's caption;
   - the Correct form's pattern buttons.

   "Colour" becomes "Coat" in the headings. The "What the public sees" box stays as it is.
2. **Cat guardians' pages (PR 2).** Guardians' own pages learn the cat's colours and pattern (today they only get the old public field), then use the same team words.

Nothing stored changes, there's no migration, and the public app doesn't change. Neither PR blocks the 31 October launch.

## Global constraints

- **One function** gives the team phrase, `teamCoatPhrase`, and one gives the pattern word, `teamPatternWord`. They live in a new `lib/teamPages/teamCoatWords.ts`, and nothing else builds a team coat phrase.
- **The golden table:** spec §15.6, all 128 rows, copied into a test fixture by hand from the spec (not generated by the code under test). The function must match it word for word.
- **English only (T4A).** The team words are produced only when the page language resolves to English (`pageLanguage(lang) === "en"`). Otherwise the page uses §2's words through the existing `coat.*` and `pattern.*` strings. The phrase grammar is English code, not catalogue strings.
- **The public never gets a team word.** `teamCoatWords.ts` must never be imported by the public routes, by `catHelpers.ts`, or by anything that builds the public payload. A source test enforces this, in the style of `position.guard.source.test.ts`.
- **Stored codes are unchanged.** The Correct form still posts `plain`, `stripes`, `patches` or `none`.
- **Words:** "cat guardian", never "colony carer"; "spotter", never "snapper"; "Ginger", never "Red".
- **Every PR:**
  - is built in its own worktree off fresh `origin/main`;
  - has a `CHECKPOINT.md` (untracked);
  - **commits after each item**;
  - runs its mutation probes and records them in the PR;
  - is reviewed through `/ship` with an adversarial reviewer in its own worktree;
  - typechecks and passes the full suite on its own.
- **No high-volume port loops.** The model is mocked, and nothing writes to production.

## PR 1: the team pages

### Task 1.1: the words (`lib/teamPages/teamCoatWords.ts`, new)

- `teamPatternWord(pattern: CoatPattern | null): string` gives "Solid", "Tabby", "Patched" or "Not known".
- `teamCoatPhrase(coat: { colours: readonly string[] | null; pattern: string | null }, gap: "known" | "stated" = "known"): string`. It follows §15.2's rules:
  - an unknown part is said as ", pattern not known" or ", colours not known";
  - with `gap: "stated"`, the same wording says "not stated" (for one statement);
  - only Black with Ginger in patches gives Tortoiseshell or Calico (T5A);
  - Black and Ginger stripes give "Black and ginger tabby" (T6A);
  - codes outside the vocabulary are dropped, as in `coatDrawing.ts`.
- `coatWordsFor(lang, coat, gap?)` is the one entry point pages call. In English it returns the team phrase; in any other language, §2's words joined with " · ".
- **Tests** (`teamCoatWords.test.ts`):
  - all 128 rows of the golden fixture (`__fixtures__/teamCoatGolden.ts`, copied from §15.6);
  - a "not stated" table for statements (at least the 12 rule rows);
  - unknown codes dropped;
  - Greek and French give §2's words (with `LIVE_LANGUAGES` widened in the test).

### Task 1.2: the cat page

- **Summary line and photo description:**
  - `catFacts.ts` `coatWordsOf` calls `coatWordsFor`;
  - the `catPages.ts` photo `alt` uses the same phrase.
- **The Appearance section** (`appearancePage.ts`):
  - the heading is "Coat" (en `appearance.colour` becomes "Coat");
  - the status rows: the pattern row uses `teamPatternWord`, and the colours row keeps the colour words;
  - each statement says its phrase with `gap: "stated"`. Its codes are split back into colours and pattern by vocabulary;
  - the drawn cat's caption is `coatWordsFor` (`coatDrawing.ts` `drawnCaption` and `aria-label`);
  - the **"What the public sees" box is unchanged** (`publicLine`).
- **The Correct form's pattern buttons (T3A):**
  - *Solid (one colour all over, maybe a little white)*;
  - *Tabby (stripes, swirls or spots)*;
  - *Patched (tortoiseshell, calico, black and white…)*;
  - *Not stated*.

  These are English team strings. Greek and French keep `pattern.*`.
- **The history** (`teamCatView.ts` `coatHistoryText`): "Coat: Ginger tabby", with the phrase for one statement ("not stated").

### Task 1.3: the Colour check list and the Cats grid

- **Colour check rows:** "Coat: {phrase}" (the label follows `appearance.colour`).
- **Cats grid** (`catGridPage.ts`):
  - the card line and `alt` use the phrase;
  - "Colour not known" becomes "Coat not known";
  - the colour filter chips are unchanged.

### Task 1.4: the public guard

- **A source test:** no file under `routes/` (except `team*.ts`), nor `lib/catHelpers.ts` and the files it imports, imports `teamCoatWords`.
- **Field by field:** in `GET /api/cats` and `GET /api/cats/:id`, `coatColours` holds only colour codes, `coatPattern` holds only pattern codes or null, and no other field carries a team phrase.
- **The public snapshot fixtures gain a striped cat**, so a leak into the payload would change the snapshot. The snapshots are regenerated on purpose, and the diff is recorded in the PR.

### Task 1.5: the documents

- Spec §15.1 already lists the places. Note in the SYC-396 plan, Task 1.4.1, that the summary line calls `coatWordsFor` (built in #228; switched here).

### PR 1 tests (in addition to Task 1.1's)
