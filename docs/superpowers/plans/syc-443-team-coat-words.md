# SYC-443 implementation plan: team screens say a coat in the old words

**Spec:** `docs/specs/coat-colours-and-pattern.md` §15, approved by Samuel on 18 October 2026 ("approved"), with T1A–T6A.
**Ticket:** SYC-443 (child of SYC-398). Folds in **SYC-436** (guardian pages), because T1A gives guardians the team words.
**Status:** **approved by Samuel, 18 October 2026** ("approve the plan, start PR 1"). Revised after an independent review (see the end).

## In plain English (for Samuel)

Two pieces of work, one after the other:

1. **Team pages (PR 1).** Every place a team page says a coat switches to tabby / tortoiseshell / calico wording, from one shared set of rules:
   - the cat page's summary line and photo description;
   - the Appearance section;
   - the history;
   - the Colour check list;
   - the Cats grid cards;
   - the drawn cat's caption;
   - the Correct form's pattern buttons.

   "Colour" becomes "Coat" in the headings and labels. The "What the public sees" box stays as it is.
2. **Cat guardians' pages (PR 2).** Guardians' own pages learn the cat's colours and pattern (today they only get the old field kept for older phones), then use the same wording.

**What people will notice:**
- the team and guardians see "Ginger tabby", "Calico", and Solid / Tabby / Patched;
- the public app keeps "Ginger · Stripes" and Plain / Stripes / Patches;
- a guardian who reads the dashboard in Greek or French, once those languages are switched on, will see the public's words in that language, not a Greek or French "tabby".

There's no database change and nothing stored changes. Neither PR blocks the 31 October launch.

**Your steps:**
- approve this plan;
- later, "review and merge" each PR;
- to put each one live, pull main in Replit's Git pane, and the coordinator publishes.

## Global constraints

**One module, `lib/teamPages/teamCoatWords.ts`:**
- `teamPatternWord(pattern)` gives "Solid", "Tabby", "Patched" or "Not known".
- `teamCoatPhrase(coat, gap)` gives the §15.2 phrase.
- `coatIsUnknown(coat)` is true when neither part is known (colours `null` or `[]`, and pattern `null`).
- `coatWordsFor(lang, coat, { gap, live })` is the one entry point pages call.
- `TEAM_PATTERN_LABELS` holds the Correct buttons' words and hints (T3A).

Nothing else builds a team coat phrase.

**English only (T4A):**
- `coatWordsFor` returns the team phrase only when `pageLanguage(lang, live) === "en"`, where `live` defaults to `LIVE_LANGUAGES` and is passed by callers that already take it (the guardian pages). Otherwise it returns §2's words through the existing `coat.*` and `pattern.*` strings, joined with " · ".
- The team phrases, pattern words and button hints are **English constants in `teamCoatWords.ts`, not catalogue keys**. So no new Greek or French text is needed, and the string parity tests are untouched.

**The golden table:**
- spec §15.6, all 128 rows, typed by hand from the spec into `lib/teamPages/__fixtures__/teamCoatGolden.ts`, never generated by the code under test;
- the function must match it word for word;
- `[]` colours count as not known, the same as `null`.

**The public never gets a team word.** A source allowlist test enforces it (Task 1.4).

**The rest:**
- **Stored codes unchanged:** the Correct form still posts `plain`, `stripes`, `patches` or `none`.
- **Words:** "cat guardian", never "colony carer"; "spotter", never "snapper"; "Ginger", never "Red".
- **Every PR:**
  - is built in its own worktree off fresh `origin/main`;
  - has a `CHECKPOINT.md` (untracked);
  - **commits after each item**;
  - runs its mutation probes and records them in the PR;
  - is reviewed through `/ship` with an adversarial reviewer in its own worktree;
  - typechecks and passes the full suite on its own.
- **No high-volume port loops.** The model is mocked, and nothing writes to production.

**Where languages reach today.** No team route passes a language:
- the cat page, Appearance, grid, Colour check and history are drawn in English whatever the person's language;
- only the guardian pages are language-aware (`routes/teamMy.ts`).

So T4A is tested by **direct render calls** with `lang` and `live` passed, and on the **guardian pages** (PR 2). It isn't tested through the team routes.

## PR 1: the team pages

### Task 1.1: the words (`lib/teamPages/teamCoatWords.ts`, new)

- **`teamCoatPhrase(coat, gap)`** follows §15.2:
  - unknown parts are said as ", pattern not known" or ", colours not known", or "not stated" with `gap: "stated"`;
  - only Black with Ginger in patches gives Tortoiseshell or Calico (T5A);
  - Black and Ginger stripes give "Black and ginger tabby" (T6A);
  - codes outside the vocabulary are dropped (as in `coatDrawing.ts`);
  - input order doesn't matter (colours are put in `COAT_COLOURS` order).
- **Tests** (`teamCoatWords.test.ts`):
  - **The golden table:** all 128 rows. A completeness check that the fixture has 128 distinct (colour set, pattern) keys covering all 32 × 4. Each row once with `[]` in place of `null`, where colours are unknown. One row given in reverse colour order.
  - **"Not stated" table** for single statements. Its cases are:
    - each pattern with colours not stated ("Tabby, colours not stated", "Solid, colours not stated", "Patched, colours not stated");
    - colours with the pattern not stated ("Ginger and white, pattern not stated", "Black, pattern not stated").
  - **`coatIsUnknown`:** `{null, null}`, `{[], null}`, and not for any known part.
  - **`coatWordsFor` with `live = ["en","el","fr"]`:** English gives the team phrase; Greek and French give §2's Greek and French words. With the default live list, Greek falls back to English.

### Task 1.2: the cat page

- **Summary line** (`catFacts.ts`):
  - the fact's label is **"Coat"** (today a hard-coded "Colour" at `catFacts.ts:145`);
  - `coatWordsOf(v)` becomes `coatPhraseOf(v): string | null`, built from `v.appearance.coat.parts` through `coatWordsFor`, and null when `coatIsUnknown`;
  - `colourFact` shows **one chip** holding the phrase, or the existing "not known" treatment when null.
- **Photo description** (`catPages.ts` ~724–728): "Photo of {name}, {phrase}", or "Photo of {name}" when the coat is unknown, never "…, Not known".
- **The Appearance section** (`appearancePage.ts`):
  - **Headings:** en `appearance.colour` becomes **"Coat"**. This changes the section heading, Confirm's "(Coat)" and "That's right: coat", and the Colour check line label. el and fr keep Χρώμα and Couleur.
  - **Status rows:** the pattern row uses `teamPatternWord` (English) and the colours row keeps the colour words.
  - **Each statement:** its phrase with `gap: "stated"`, its codes split back into colours and pattern by vocabulary. The vocabularies don't overlap, and old codes are already translated by `readCoatValue` before `codesOf`.
  - **Drawn cat:** caption and `aria-label` from `coatWordsFor` (`coatDrawing.ts` `drawnCaption`).
  - **"What the public sees":** unchanged (`publicLine`).
  - **The Correct form's pattern buttons:** `TEAM_PATTERN_LABELS` in English: *Solid (one colour all over, maybe a little white)*, *Tabby (stripes, swirls or spots)*, *Patched (tortoiseshell, calico, black and white…)*, *Not stated*. In Greek and French: `pattern.*`, as now.
- **The history** (`teamCatView.ts`):
  - `coatHistoryText` calls `teamCoatPhrase(parts, "stated")`, so a statement reads "Coat: Ginger tabby". `teamCatView.ts` may import `teamCoatWords` (allowlisted).
  - The "not sure", "unknown" and "(agrees with #n)" branches are kept.
  - A statement with neither part still says "unknown".
  - **The team JSON `history[].said` changes** on purpose, from "brown and ginger, stripes" to "Brown and ginger tabby". It stays English-only, as today.

### Task 1.3: the Colour check list and the Cats grid

- **Colour check rows:** "Coat: {phrase}" (`appearancePage.ts` `checkRow`; the label follows `appearance.colour`).
- **Cats grid** (`catGridPage.ts`):
  - the card line and photo description use the phrase;
  - an unknown coat says **"Coat not known"** and the description leaves the coat out;
  - the colour filter chips, the colour words and the addresses are unchanged.

### Task 1.4: the public guard

- **Source allowlist** (`teamCoatWords.guard.source.test.ts`, in the style of `position.guard.source.test.ts`):
  - only `lib/teamPages/**`, `lib/teamCatView.ts` and `routes/team*.ts` may import `teamCoatWords`;
  - it matches `from`, `import(` and `require(`, with or without `.js`;
  - it asserts the scan read a non-empty set of files.
- **Field checks:** extend `routes/cats.appearance.public.test.ts`, which already seeds striped cats. For every cat in `GET /api/cats` and `GET /api/cats/:id`:
  - `coatColours` is a subset of the five colour codes;
  - `coatPattern` is one of `plain`, `stripes`, `patches` or null;
  - the old `coat` stays within its seven codes, as today.
- **Not done:** no whole-payload text search, because the old `coat` legitimately holds "tabby" and cat names can be "Calico". No snapshot change.

## PR 2: cat guardians' pages (SYC-436 under T1A)

**Depends on:** PR 1 merged.

- **`GuardianCat`** (`guardianCatView.ts`) gains `coatColours` and `coatPattern`, read from `status.coatColours` and `status.coatPattern` (`catHelpers.ts`). An empty `coatColours` (`[]`) means not known. The old `coat` stays on the view for now.
- **`guardianPages.ts` `appearanceLine`:** the coat part is `coatWordsFor(lang, { colours, pattern }, { live })`, then " · ", then the marks through `markShownWord`, as today. When the coat is unknown (`coatIsUnknown`), only the marks are shown. `legacyPublicLine` is removed. There is no separate "what the public sees" line on guardian pages, so `publicLine` isn't used here.
- **The old strings:**
  - `coat.tabby`, `coat.tortoiseshell_or_calico` and `coat.black_and_white` are removed from `teamPages/strings/{en,el,fr}.ts`;
  - in the same change, the legacy branch of `coatWord` (`appearancePage.ts` ~36–43) is removed, so nothing reaches for a missing key;
  - `coat.black`, `coat.white`, `coat.ginger` and `coat.grey` are shared and stay;
  - the string parity test stays green.
- **Tests:**
  - a guardian page in English shows "Ginger tabby" and the marks;
  - with Greek live (`live` passed, as the guardian tests already do), it shows "Πορτοκαλί · Ρίγες";
  - it never shows "Tortoiseshell or calico";
  - an unknown coat shows only the marks;
  - the guardian scope rules are unchanged.
- **Mutation probe:** `appearanceLine` falls back to the old codes. Expect red: the English guardian phrase test.
- **Browser check:** a guardian's cat page at 390 px and desktop, in English, and in Greek through the harness.
