# Pre-MVP tester survey (Syros, September 2026)

Five questions sent to the Syros Cats testers after the pre-MVP testing window.
Rendered version: `docs/tester-survey.html` (also published as an artifact).

Scope check: the questions cover only what the tested build actually does —
photograph a cat, match or register it, name it, catalogue, map, points /
badges / leaderboard, offline queue, English / Greek / French. There is no
welfare-report question because the cat-in-trouble flow is not in this build.

## 1. Which of these did you manage to do? (multi-select, required)

- Took a photo of a cat and recorded the sighting
- Confirmed a cat the app already knew
- Registered a brand new cat and named it
- Looked through the cat catalogue
- Looked at the map
- Checked my points, badges or the leaderboard
- Used it somewhere with no signal or bad signal
- Used it in Greek
- I opened it but never recorded a cat
- I could not get it to open or install at all

Measures how far down the loop each tester got; the last two options are the
drop-at-the-door count.

## 2. How easy was it to record one cat, from opening the app to finishing? (1–5, required)

1 Very hard · 2 Hard · 3 All right · 4 Easy · 5 Very easy

The one trackable number. Repeat it a month after launch.

## 3. Did any of these happen to you? (multi-select, required + optional long text)

- It said there was no cat in the photo when there clearly was
- It treated a cat I know is already in the app as a new cat
- It suggested a cat that was obviously a different cat
- The location was wrong, or it would not pick up my location
- The camera or the photo would not work
- It was slow, froze, or closed itself
- I lost a sighting I thought I had saved
- I was not sure what to do next
- The wording was confusing, or the Greek was wrong
- Nothing went wrong

Free text: "Tell us what happened, in as much or as little detail as you like."

The first three options separate the two matching failures (missing a known
cat vs offering a wrong one).

## 4. If we could only fix or add one thing before the app goes live in October, what should it be? (short text, required)

Forced single answer, so the testers do the ranking.

## 5. Would you recommend it to someone else on Syros who cares about the cats? (single choice, required + optional short text)

- Yes — I already have
- Yes
- Not yet, but I would once it is finished
- No

Free text: "What would have to change for that to be a clear yes?"

## Running it

- Two separate forms, English and Greek, both links in one WhatsApp message.
  A bilingual single form costs a question and invites mis-ticked options.
- No email or name field. Email capture is a designed in-app moment; a
  feedback form is the wrong place for it.
- The Greek needs a native read before sending (folds into the open
  Greek/French review item).
- Open for a week, one reminder only.

## Reading the answers

1. Count Q1's two failure options first — that is the share of motivated
   people the app lost at the door.
2. Read Q3's first three options against the number who actually recorded
   cats, not the total.
3. Three or more testers naming the same thing in Q4 is a launch blocker;
   scattered singletons go to the backlog.
4. A low Q2 score from someone who ticked plenty in Q1 pushed through
   difficulty instead of giving up — interview them directly.
